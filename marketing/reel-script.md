# Lucid: launch reel (final cut spec)

One video. No voiceover. Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps delivery, shot at 4K60. Runtime 22.5 s. Same file posted to TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and X, with a different caption and link strategy per platform (bottom of this file).

Changes from the first draft, all applied below: competitor paywall shot removed (legal risk, and the new hook says the same thing in four words), voiceover removed, reactions scene removed (no footage), CTA changed to "Comment LUCID", new hook, anti-slop rules added.

---

## The rules that keep this from looking like AI slop

Short-form viewers can smell a template in half a second. These are the tells, and the video avoids every one of them.

- **No synthetic voice.** Nothing says "I made this in 20 minutes" faster than an AI narrator. Text and sound only.
- **No AI-generated footage.** Every frame is a real screen, a real room, or a real screen recording. You have a Higgsfield connector; do not use it for b-roll here. The product is real footage of a real thing. That's the whole flex.
- **No auto-captions.** No bouncing word-by-word captions, no yellow highlight, no emoji. Static typeset text, cut in on the frame, held, cut out.
- **No CapCut transitions.** Hard cuts and one dip to black. Nothing zooms, spins, glitches or flashes.
- **No stock whoosh library.** Sub hits you make yourself, a keyboard you record yourself, transients you can barely hear.
- **No lifted blacks, no teal/orange.** Neutral, dark, slightly crushed.
- **No stock music with a vocal chop.** Minimal piano/pad, one open moment, ends in silence.
- **The camera shots carry the video.** A real MacBook in a dark room, filmed on a tripod, is what reads as expensive. Screen recordings are evidence, not beauty.

## Music

One track, no vocals, no drums until the drop. 90–110 BPM so the 0.75 s catalog cuts fall on beats.

| Section | Time | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Silence | 00:00 – 00:02.0 | Sub hits only. |
| Hold | 00:02.0 – 00:10 | One sustained piano note or warm pad, minor key. Second quiet layer enters at 00:06. |
| Rise | 00:10 – 00:14.5 | Low-pass riser, tension, no resolution. |
| Drop | 00:14.5 | Full arrangement on the Apply cut. Chord progression opens. Kick or sub pulse. |
| Ride | 00:14.5 – 00:19.8 | Full through the catalog. |
| Resolve | 00:19.8 – 00:21.5 | One held note. |
| Silence | 00:21.5 – 00:22.5 | Nothing. |

Free sources, in order of how clean the license is:

| Source | License | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Pixabay Music | Commercial OK, no attribution | Search "minimal piano cinematic", "ambient tech". Start here. |
| YouTube Audio Library | Free; some tracks need a credit (shown per track) | Filter Cinematic / Calm. Most tracks are cleared for use off YouTube. |
| Uppbeat | Free with a credit line in the caption | Good curated cinematic. |
| Free Music Archive | Per track; use only CC BY or CC0 | Skip anything NC. |

Trending sounds: don't. A business or creator account promoting a product with a trending commercial track is outside the license on both platforms. Posting from a TikTok Business account (which you want anyway, see below) locks you to the Commercial Music Library. You're using a free track regardless, so there's no loss.

Practical: find three candidates on Pixabay, lay each under the rough cut, keep the one where the drop lands at 00:14.5 with the least stretching.

Free SFX: Freesound.org (filter License: CC0), Pixabay Sound Effects, Mixkit.

### Mix targets

- −14 LUFS integrated, −1 dBTP peaks. Both platforms normalize around −14; louder gets squashed.
- High-pass everything except the sub hits at 80 Hz.
- Check on a phone speaker. If the hook thump disappears, layer a 120 Hz hit under it; phone speakers reproduce the harmonic, not the fundamental.

## Shooting checklist

**Mac prep**
- Clean desktop, Dock hidden (⌘⌥D), desktop icons hidden, Focus mode on.
- Brightness 70–80 %. At 100 % the camera clips the screen.
- Terminal: dark theme, 18 pt, scrollback cleared, install line pre-pasted.
- Pre-download every wallpaper you'll show. The video is about Apply, not download.
- Screen record with ⌘⇧5 at native resolution.
- No Apple Aerials anywhere on screen. Personal-use license; not for a promo.

**Camera**
- Tripod. 4K60. Manual exposure locked, shutter 1/60 or 1/120. Auto exposure will pump when the desktop changes and ruin S04.
- 20–30° off-axis to kill reflections and moiré.
- Dark room, one warm practical behind or beside the Mac.
- Wipe the screen.

## Assembly

Crop a 16:9 4K screen recording to a vertical region:

```bash
# x,y = top-left of the 1080x1920 window inside the 3840x2160 recording
ffmpeg -i terminal.mov -vf "crop=1080:1920:1380:120" -c:v libx264 -crf 16 -r 30 terminal_v.mp4
```

Never upscale a crop more than 1.3×. If the region is too small, re-record with bigger UI.

Export: H.264, 1080×1920, 30 fps, 12–16 Mbps, AAC 256 kbps.

## Link strategy per platform

Your read on the platforms is partly wrong, and it matters because the CTA depends on it.

| Platform | Link reality | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | Bio link works. DMs with links work. | Set up a free-tier ManyChat (or Instagram's built-in comment-to-DM automation if your account has it) so "LUCID" in the comments triggers an automatic DM with the GitHub link. Reply manually to the first ten anyway; it trains the algorithm and looks human. |
| **TikTok** | Personal accounts get a clickable bio link at 1,000 followers. **Business accounts get it immediately at any follower count.** DMs containing links are frequently filtered or blocked, especially to people who don't follow you back. Comments can't hold clickable links. | Switch to a Business account today. You get the bio link, and since you're using free music anyway you lose nothing. Then "Comment LUCID" still runs for the engagement, and your reply to each comment is "link in bio, or search Lucid Live Wallpaper on GitHub." Pin a comment saying the same thing before the video gets traffic. Do not rely on DMs for delivery on TikTok. Record one "reply with video" to the first good comment; it pushes the original. |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Links in the description and in a pinned comment work. | Pin the raw GitHub link. Keep "Comment LUCID" in the video for the engagement signal; the link is already there for anyone who looks. |
| **X** | Posts with a link in the body get less reach. | Video in the post, link in the first reply. "Comment LUCID" works fine here too. |
| **Reddit** | Link in the post. Reddit hates ads. | Don't post the reel as a reel. Post the install line, the GitHub link, a screenshot, and one sentence in r/macapps and r/MacOS: "I built a free open-source live 4K wallpaper app for Mac, MIT licensed, no account, no tracking." Answer every comment. The reel can go in a comment if someone asks for a demo. |

The honest version of the CTA across all of this: "Comment LUCID" gets you comments everywhere, and gets the link to Instagram users automatically. Everywhere else the link is already in the bio, description or reply, and your comment replies point to it. Don't promise a DM on TikTok you can't deliver.

One risk to know about: Meta has said it reduces reach on posts it classifies as engagement bait. "Comment X for a link" is at the edge of that. It still works in practice, but keep the CTA to one line, don't repeat it in the caption, and don't put it in the first three seconds. It's at the end, where it belongs.

## What will kill this video

- A speed-ramped install.
- Apple Aerials on screen.
- A synthetic voice.
- AI-generated b-roll.
- A blurred vertical background behind a 16:9 recording.
- Auto-captions.
- Any transition that isn't a hard cut or a dip to black.
- Promising a TikTok DM you can't send.
